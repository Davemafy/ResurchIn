# ResurchIn landing redesign

## Direction
The brand stays recognizable through the off-black field, square dotted `r` mark, serif editorial voice, research notation and one warm ochre. The page no longer relies on the floating dossier, sticky-note accents, halftone filler, ghost numbers or repeated yellow CTAs.

## What changed
1. Yellow is limited to the brand mark, the active revision trace, and the admissions close. Focus/selection also use it for accessibility.
2. Brand yellow is warmed to `#C6A037`.
3. The hero dossier is replaced by a product-native revision trace showing V01, V03 and V06 of the same question.
4. The sample study contains a full question, evidence note, sample specification, revised claim and limit. It is explicitly labeled fictional training content.
5. The old equal Frame/Test/Defend row is replaced by an uneven six-stage line. Stage buttons update one shared detail panel.
6. Hero copy describes the actual six-month loop: frame, read, choose a method, analyse and revise sentence-level claims with mentor review.
7. Instrument Serif remains the display face, but emphasis comes from scale, line rhythm and switching between serif/sans/mono rather than one giant italic line.
8. Decorative filler is removed. The dot field survives only inside the brand mark because it carries identity.

## Interaction
- V01 / V03 / V06 question trace is clickable and accessible as tabs.
- Six programme stages are clickable and accessible as tabs.
- Mobile navigation is a simple menu, not a fullscreen effects layer.
- No custom cursor, parallax, scroll-jacking or scroll-bound transforms.
- Reduced-motion mode disables transitions.

## Responsive intent
- Desktop: 2-column hero with revision trace as the signature object.
- 768 class: hero becomes stacked, process becomes a 2-column sequence.
- 390 class: typography, trace and sample content collapse cleanly to one column.
- 320 class: additional font and spacing reductions keep all content inside the viewport.

Inner routes are untouched.
