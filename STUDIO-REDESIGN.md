# ResurchIn landing page redesign

## Direction: Reopening

The homepage is rebuilt around one human idea: a question that keeps pulling you back.

### What changed
- retained the ResurchIn dark field, ochre mark, dotted-r identity and wordmark
- warmed the yellow to #C99A2E and replaced pure black/white with charcoal and paper
- added ink blue #17353B as the supporting neutral
- rebuilt the hero around a real image rather than a document mockup
- reduced yellow to deliberate moments rather than a universal accent
- changed the old equal process cards into an uneven six-stage research line
- kept the V01/V06 revision interaction, but made it a compact editorial strip attached to the photograph
- added real sample claim text instead of placeholder bars
- rebuilt mobile as a different composition, including a real menu and horizontally scrollable process line
- replaced the generic footer with a large cropped brand mark and short metadata rail
- removed the custom cursor and decorative filler

### Type
- Bricolage Grotesque for display
- DM Sans for body/navigation
- DM Mono for research metadata
